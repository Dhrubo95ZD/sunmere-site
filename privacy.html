Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026.

What we collect

If you sign in with Google, the game receives your Google account id and display name through our sign-in provider (Supabase). Your email address is handled only by the sign-in provider; the game server never shows it to other players.

The game server stores your characters (name, class, level, items and progress) linked to your account id.

The browser version is served by GitHub Pages. The online world server runs on the developer's own computer: like any web server it sees the network address you connect from, which is used only to keep the connection working and to limit abuse (it is not stored with your characters).

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your data, show ads, or track you across other sites. Offline play sends nothing anywhere.

Deleting your data

You can delete your characters on the login screen. To have your account removed entirely, contact the developer through GitHub (Dhrubo95ZD) and it will be deleted.
